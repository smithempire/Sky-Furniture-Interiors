Our Story

Built On Wood, Grown Through Trust

Sky Furnitures and Interior began with a simple belief: every space deserves furniture that is both beautiful and built to last. What started as a small workshop in Owerri has grown into a trusted name for furniture construction and interior finishing across Nigeria.

Today we serve homes, offices, hotels, restaurants, schools and churches — designing, building, installing, repairing, delivering and customizing furniture and interior solutions for every kind of space.

Our reputation is built one satisfied client at a time, and every project still gets the same hands-on attention as our very first commission.
